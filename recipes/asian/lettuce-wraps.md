### Description

A PF _Changs/Pei Wei favorite,_ consists of ground pork simmered in asian sauce and served wrapped in lettuce leaves.

### Ingredients:

#### Stir Fry

* 1t toasted sesame oil
* 1T olive oil
* 1lb ground chicken or pork
* med onion, finely diced
* 2 cloves minced garlic
* 1T minced ginger
* 8 ounce can water chestnuts, drained and diced
* 2 scallions, thinly sliced
* 1 head butter lettuce

#### Sauce

Amounts are approximate.  Tune to your own tastes

* 1/4c hoisin sauce
* 2T light soy sauce
* 2T rice wine
* 1t cornstarch

### Equipment

* Large skillet

### Method

#### Prep Sauce

1. Mix all sauce ingredients into a cup.
1. Set aside

#### Meat mixture

1. Heat sesame oil over medium heat
1. Add ground chicken/pork, breaking it up until cooked thoroughly &mdash; about 5 to 7 minutes.
1. Add onion, garlic, and ginger.  Sauté an additional 3 to 4 minutes or until onions are soft.
1. Add sauce, simmer over low heat for ~30 minutes.


### Notes

1. We like ground pork.
1. The recipe calls for butter lettuce, mainly because the leaves are well formed and easy to separate into cups.  We've used iceberg as well &mdash; the leaves don't separate as easily, but they're nice and crunchy.
1. Great for leftovers.  Like most spice mixtures, it's usually better the next day.
1. Olive oil seems an odd ingredient for an asian recipe.
