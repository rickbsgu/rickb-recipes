### Description


This is a really chunky baked granola made with an assortment of seeds, oats, honey, maple syrup, and cinnamon.

Its' a great topper for oatmeal, or just eat it with some yogurt.

### Tools

Baking sheet

### Ingredients

* 4c Rolled oats
* 1c Pumpkin seeds
* 1c Sliced almonds
* 1/2c Sunflower seeds
* 1/2c Flax seeds
* 1/4c Sesame seeds
* 1/2c Coconut flakes
* 1/4c Honey
* 1/4c Maple syrup
* 1/4c Coconut oil
* 1T Ground cinnamon
* 1T Vanilla extract
* 1/4t Salt

### Method

1. Heat oven to 350˚F
2. Combine seeds in a large bowl.
3. Sprinkle cinnamon over the mix.
4. Gently heat salt, maple syrup, oil, honey and vanilla extract until thin: ~5 mins.
5. Pour over dry ingredients and stir to well coat the mixture.
6. Spread mixture onto a parchment-lined pan.
7. Bake for 12 mins.
8. Turning mixture with a wide spatula.
9. Bake for another 5-10 mins (until golden brown and fragrant.)
10. When cool, store in airtight container.
