### Description

I have to admit it &mdash; when it comed to teriyaki sauce, I usually head for the store.

Per usual, store-bought teriyaki is loaded with sugar/HFCS, so it's not an ideal solution.  And, it's pricey, comparatively.

Found this recipe on the _Daring Gourmet_ and decided to give it a try.

### Ingredients

* 1/2C tamari (see notes)
* 1/4C coconut sugar
* 1-1/2t grated ginger
* 1t minced garlic
* 1T honey
* 1t sesame oil
* 3T rice wine
* 1/4c water
* 3t cornstarch

### Method

1. Combine all ingredients in a small saucepan, bring to a boil.
1. Reduce head and simmer for about 4 minutes.

### Notes

1. Tamari is like concentrated soy sauce.  It's also gluten free, which soy sauce is not.  You can get it on Amazon.
1. You can use other liquid sweeteners in place of the honey, like agave.
